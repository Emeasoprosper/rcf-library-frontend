Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

RCF MOUAU Digital Library ("the App") is a resource-sharing platform for Michael Okpara University of Agriculture, Umudike (MOUAU) students, staff, and affiliates. This page explains what information we collect and how it's used.

Information We Collect

  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account.
  • During profile completion, we collect your department, academic level, and (for MOUAU students) your student ID.
  • When you upload, download, bookmark, request, or read a resource, we record that activity to power features like reading history, recommendations, and download management.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate your account and authenticate you securely.
  • To let you upload and access library resources.
  • To show your name and department to library administrators reviewing your submissions.
  • To generate personalized recommendations and reading history.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Sign-In for authentication and Google Drive for secure file storage of library materials. We use Google's Gemini AI to help detect metadata (title, author, subject) from uploaded documents — this analysis happens automatically and does not share your personal account information with the AI service, only the content of the document being uploaded.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Your name and department may be visible to other users on resources you contribute, unless you choose to upload anonymously.

Your Choices

You may request account deletion or data removal by contacting us using the email below.
